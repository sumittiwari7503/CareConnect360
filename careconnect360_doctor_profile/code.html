About Dr. Vance

Dr. Elena Vance, MD, is a nationally recognized, double-boarded dermatologist specializing in both clinical outpatient dermatology and digital teledermatology. With over 14 years of clinical leadership, Dr. Vance treats complex inflammatory skin diseases, acute dermatoses, acne vulgaris, and pediatric conditions.

Her patient-first philosophy focuses on proactive, barrier-restoring dermatologic health and accessible virtual consultations. In addition to her active clinical load at Metro Health Medical Center, she directs the Digital Tele-Dermatology Triage Program, training resident physicians to harness high-resolution clinical imaging for expedited diagnostics.
